abstract: >
  We prepared a three-electron fermionic Laughlin state on a superconducting quantum processor and reconstructed its
  density matrix. The reconstruction revealed vortex-dressed coherence extending beyond electron coherence.
  We prepared a quasihole, benchmarked its fractional charge response, and used controlled quasihole motion to
  extract a geometric-phase signature.
