tags: [Driven-Dissipative Systems, Bose-Hubbard Lattice]

abstract: >
  Spatially structured dissipation organizes driven quantum matter beyond Hamiltonian control. Here we show
  that a dissipation gradient combined with a detuning that increases linearly with position selects a
  nonlinear front in a two-dimensional driven-dissipative Bose-Hubbard lattice, producing a pinned density
  profile. Varying the gradients changes the front profile and its stability, with abrupt rearrangements
  producing steps in the total particle number. Center-of-mass and transverse-momentum diagnostics map the
  front dynamics, revealing a dynamical phase diagram comprising a pinned regime with uniform or
  pattern-locked fronts, spatiotemporal chaos, and minimum-loss localization. Our results establish structured
  dissipation as a mechanism for reconfigurable transport barriers and programmable nonequilibrium interfaces
  in bosonic lattices, with direct implications for quantum simulators based on photonic, polaritonic, and
  superconducting-circuit platforms.
